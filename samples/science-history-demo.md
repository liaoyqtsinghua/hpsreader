# Science Revolution Demo

Hypothesis was not merely a guess in the early modern debate. Hypothesis often marked a disciplined proposal about hidden causes, mathematical order, and the limits of demonstration.

Newton rejected speculative Hypothesis when it claimed more than phenomena could warrant. Yet the same debate shows that Hypothesis remained useful when it organized experiments and clarified the scope of natural philosophy.

Space was another unstable term. Space could name extension, container, relation, or the measurable order in which bodies appear.

The translator must avoid treating Space as a single modern concept. A consistent glossary should record when Space is rendered as 空间, 容器, or 广延.
